About the researcher

Evidence, methods and demographic insight.

Samuel Oluwaseun Adeyemo is a Principal Lecturer in Mathematics and Statistics at Federal Polytechnic Nekede, Owerri, Nigeria, and a PhD researcher in Mathematical Demography.

His research focuses on fertility and mortality analysis, demographic estimation, survey statistics, multilevel modelling, small-area estimation, reproductive health, actuarial science and applied mathematics.

He welcomes collaboration in demography, statistical modelling, survey methodology and data analytics.
